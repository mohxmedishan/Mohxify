Private by design

Your dictionary belongs to you.

Mohxify is designed to process replacements on your device. Its core text-replacement features do not need an account or an internet connection.

Accessibility, explained

Mohxify uses Android Accessibility to detect completed words in editable fields and request replacements. Password fields are skipped. Some apps use custom editors that may not support replacement.
