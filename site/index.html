Private by design

Your dictionary belongs to you.

Mohxify stores rules on your device. Its core replacement workflow does not need an account or internet access.

◈

Accessibility, explained.

Android Accessibility lets Mohxify detect completed words in supported editable fields and request replacements. Password fields are skipped. Some apps use custom editors that may not support text replacement. You stay in control of the service.
