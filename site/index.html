Private by design

Your dictionary belongs to you.

Mohxify is designed to process replacements on your device. It does not need an account or an internet connection for its core text-replacement features.

What to know about Accessibility

Mohxify uses Android Accessibility to detect completed words in editable fields and request replacements. That permission is powerful, so only enable the service if you trust the app. Password fields are skipped. Some apps use custom editors that may not support replacement.

Good to know

Quick answers.

Does Mohxify replace my keyboard?

No. It is designed to work alongside your existing keyboard using Android's accessibility text-editing actions.

Does it send what I type to a server?

The current app does not request the INTERNET permission. Replacement rules and processing stay on-device.

Will it work in every app?

Not necessarily. Apps can use custom text editors or restrict accessibility text actions. Test it in a notes field first.

Can I back up my dictionaries?

Yes. The app includes JSON backup support in its storage layer and text export for individual loadouts. Keep a backup before reinstalling or changing signing keys.
